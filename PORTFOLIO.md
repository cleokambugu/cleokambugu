# Portfolio atlas

This atlas pools the products, prototypes, research, and technical infrastructure behind Cleopatra Kambugu's work. It is organised by purpose rather than by which AI workspace or hosting platform happened to contain an artifact.

Status reflects evidence reviewed on **31 August 2026**:

- **Live** — the public experience was reached and inspected.
- **Preview** — a public address exists, but access is gated.
- **Release candidate** — locally built and verified; public operations remain gated.
- **Prototype** — a functioning local or private build, not a production claim.
- **Research** — an active decision system, report, framework, or design exploration.
- **Private infrastructure** — supports the work but is not being offered as a public product or open-source release.

## What is intentionally not claimed

- A named project is not necessarily public, open source, deployed, or production-ready.
- Local test evidence does not prove real-world adoption, safety, profitability, or regulatory approval.
- AI tools helped research, design, implement, test, and document this body of work; they do not replace human authorship, responsibility, consent, or domain review.
- Upstream clones and unmodified templates are not presented as original portfolio projects.

## Collaboration

The portfolio is open to users, testers, translators, domain specialists, designers, engineers, researchers, funders, and organisations with a concrete problem. Start with the problem, the people affected, and the evidence we should respect.
