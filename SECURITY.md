# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report suspected vulnerabilities through [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/cleokambugu/cleokambugu/security/advisories/new). Do not open a public issue for a security concern.

Include, where safely possible:

- the affected file, feature, or linked public experience;
- what you observed and when;
- minimal reproduction steps;
- the likely impact and who could be affected;
- any suggested mitigation.

Do not include credentials, personal records, third-party secrets, or data obtained without permission. Do not access other people's information, disrupt services, bypass payment or identity controls, or conduct destructive testing.

## Scope and expectations

This profile repository contains portfolio documentation alongside existing technical material. A project named in the portfolio may be public, private, experimental, or research-only. Its appearance here does not authorize testing of private systems or imply a bug-bounty programme.

Good-faith reports will be evaluated against the evidence available for the affected system. Public disclosure should be coordinated only after the issue and an appropriate mitigation have been understood.
