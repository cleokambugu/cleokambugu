# Contributing

Thank you for wanting to build with us. Collaboration here starts with a real problem, the people affected, and the evidence or lived knowledge we should respect.

Participation is governed by the [community code of conduct](./.github/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Ways to contribute

- Use or test a public experience and report what happened.
- Bring domain knowledge, research, or a community perspective.
- Improve accessibility, translation, documentation, design, or code.
- Propose an institutional, funding, or implementation partnership.
- Identify a problem that deserves a focused tool.

Use the structured forms to [propose a collaboration](https://github.com/cleokambugu/cleokambugu/issues/new?template=collaboration.yml) or [report a public bug](https://github.com/cleokambugu/cleokambugu/issues/new?template=public-bug.yml).

## Before posting publicly

- Do not include passwords, API keys, private links, personal records, confidential documents, or information you do not have permission to share.
- Describe people and communities as they describe themselves. Treat identity and permission to disclose it as separate questions.
- Separate what you directly observed from what you infer, prefer, or still need to verify.
- Do not claim that a prototype is deployed, production-ready, safe, profitable, licensed, or approved without current evidence.
- For a security concern, use [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/cleokambugu/cleokambugu/security/advisories/new), not a public issue.

## A useful proposal includes

1. The problem and who experiences it.
2. What is already known or observed.
3. What you can contribute.
4. Important constraints: access, language, privacy, safety, rights, time, or cost.
5. What a small, testable first outcome would look like.

## Source and access boundaries

The [portfolio atlas](./PORTFOLIO.md) includes public products, private prototypes, research, and internal infrastructure. Being named in the atlas does not mean a system is open source, publicly deployed, or available for unrestricted testing. Access and source-release decisions are made project by project after rights, privacy, security, licensing, and operational review.

## Working agreement

We value respectful disagreement, clear provenance, reversible decisions, accessible communication, and consent-aware collaboration. Harassment, coercion, identity-based hostility, unsafe testing, fabricated evidence, or attempts to obtain protected data are not welcome.
