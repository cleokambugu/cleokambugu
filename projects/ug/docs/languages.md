# Languages: language justice in UG

UG speaks six languages: **English** and **Swahili** in full, and one language from each region
of Uganda for the words a person meets first: **Luganda** (Central and the south around
Masaka), **Runyankore-Rukiga** (the West and South-west, Mbarara to Kabale), **Acholi** (the
North, Gulu and beyond) and **Ateso** (the East, Soroti and Teso). The picker is in the nav and
in Me; the choice is remembered on the device and, when signed in, on the account.

## The honest rule

A language card shows its confidence. English and Swahili are complete. The four Ugandan
languages were drafted by the build, not by native speakers, and are marked **draft** or
**early draft**. Where a string is missing the app shows English rather than a guess. Every
Me screen carries "Review this language" so a speaker can send corrections; the dictionary is
one JSON object (`data/languages.json`, exported from the site) and a review is a pull request.

Language justice is not a menu of flags. It is: the verbs in your language on the first
screen, the doors in your language, the button that books in your language, and an app that
does not pretend fluency it lacks. It is also the next list.

## Why these six, and what is next

- Luganda is the most spoken language in the country and the language of Kampala's streets.
- Runyankore-Rukiga covers Mbarara, Bushenyi, Kabale and Kisoro, the tourism south-west.
- Acholi carries Gulu and the northern corridor; Lango (Lira) is close and next.
- Ateso carries Soroti and Teso; the East also speaks Lusoga (Jinja, Busoga's sugar belt) and
  Lumasaba (Mbale, Elgon), both on the next list because Jinja is a launch corridor.
- Swahili is the East African bridge, the bus and border language, and a national language.
- Next, in this order: Lusoga, Lango, Lumasaba, Lugbara (Arua, West Nile), Rutooro (Fort
  Portal), Ngakarimojong (Moroto and Kotido).

## What is translated now

The verbs and their flip lines, the positioning line, the two doors, the primary actions
(Get started, Book on UG, Take a seat, Call ahead, Listed, Coming, Install), the navigation and
tab bar, and the language card itself. The compare desk, the Atlas and the documents stay in
English until reviewers have signed off the core; a half-translated price screen is worse than
an English one.

## Voice

Voice commands and spoken replies are English only in this build. Speech recognition for
Luganda and Swahili exists in some engines; the roadmap adds them once the text strings are
reviewed.
