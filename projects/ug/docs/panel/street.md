# The street: a stage chairman and a Ntinda mother on UG

## The chairman's view

My riders would download it, because they download everything. They would not stay. Look at your own sum: a Jinja run in a RAV4 is UGX 70,400 of fuel and wear; four seats, three paying, the driver "keeps" 52,800 and eats 17,600 himself. That is not earning, that is a man sharing his petrol with strangers. Your concept says a driver "can be earning within minutes". A cost-share and a wage are not the same thing, and you should not write both.

The 10% at a stage means nothing until it is shillings. Faras says 10%, SafeBoda says 15%, and my boys still argue about it every evening, because what they feel is the phone credit, the MoMo withdrawal charge, and the helmet they were made to buy. Your own onboarding note says show the fee per trip in shillings. Your site says "ten percent of the rider share to keep the lights on." Whose lights?

"Vouch" — two members of a circle. At a stage the vouch is me. SafeBoda got that right: their man came to the stage, asked me, and the rider paid for training and two helmets, so he had skin in it. What they got wrong: the price pressure made boys ride fast, and the app never asked where a man is comfortable. If UG lets a chairman vouch in-app and pays him something for it, I sign my stage tomorrow. If "circles" means Stanbic and Watoto, that is not my stage.

A Comfort Map must protect a driver from: night offers he cannot refuse without a rating drop; the CBD boda-free zones; Entebbe at 03:30; and a "funded car" that pays less than the street. Your note promises decline-without-penalty; the concept does not. Put it in writing.

Cash or MoMo: riders pay cash when they sit. Your plan collects the whole fare, pays 3.5% to Flutterwave, then pays the driver "later". A boda man wants it before the rider's foot leaves the peg.

## Her view

Pre-pay a seat to Jinja with strangers because their plates are verified? No. I would pre-pay with Brenda from the office because I know Brenda. A "Stanbic circle" is only useful if it is really the people in my building, and the 17:30 Friday departure reaches Jinja at nine at night. Nobody in Kampala plans a Friday evening on the Jinja road.

"Look the part. Pay the smart price." says: you are pretending. I already know I am stretching; I don't need an app to wink at me. "Boujee" is what my nephew calls a girl who is trying too hard. I would never tell the accountant I subscribe to Boujee. And "airport lounge-style waiting room" — at Entebbe pick-up? Where?

The compare screen: yes, that I would use. Every morning I open Bolt and SafeBoda and pick. One screen is real. But your Uber rates are from 2020 and Bolt is "modelled"; if the quote is wrong twice I stop trusting the whole app. I want a "what people paid this week on Ntinda–Nakasero" number, not a formula.

Bwindi: nine hours, three children, a permit you must book months ahead. I would plan Ssese or Jinja. Bwindi is for foreigners and for my forties. The planner is nice, but "pooled transport by default" for a family weekend is not how families move.

What I fear: a 03:30 airport pool with men I don't know; a refund at the cut-off that takes a week and comes back minus MoMo charges; twelve cakes on a boda "with box".

## Copy that lands and copy that insults

Lands: "Every ride in Kampala, quoted on one screen." "The Prado is a group decision." "Paid the way Kampala pays: mobile money." "Someone in your circle is already driving." "Arrive properly."

Insults: "warns you where a boda is faster than your dignity is worth" — I take a boda every day, with dignity. "No awkward cash" — cash is not awkward, it is how we pay the driver. "Boujee". "Look the part." "Helmet included" as a sub-line for SafeBoda, as if a helmet is a feature and not the law. "Kwanjula grade" on the V8 — the V8 comes from an uncle, and it is the groom's side that pays.

## Ten things UG does not understand about Kampala

1. A stage is a chairman and a list, not a queue of strangers with a fill bar.
2. The boda price is agreed per trip; a quote is a starting point, not a fare.
3. Rain does not mean +10%; it means no bodas and a wet wait.
4. Nobody pre-pays a stranger. You pay when you sit and see the face.
5. MoMo withdrawal charges are real money; the driver counts them, your model doesn't.
6. Bolt's office visit is not a bottleneck; it is how a driver knows the company exists.
7. The function car comes from family or a known special-hire man on WhatsApp, not a yard.
8. Special-hire drivers are personal drivers; you keep their number for years.
9. The CBD boda-free zone means the boda drops you at the edge and you walk.
10. Nobody here has heard of Felt, Infrared City, Tazama or Cephable.

## Recommendations for v2

1. Show a driver, in shillings, what he takes home on each offer, and price a margin above cost — or stop saying "earning".
2. Make the stage chairman a first-class vouch account with a cut per rider he signs.
3. Authorise on MoMo and charge when the rider is seated; refunds instant and fee-free.
4. Kill "dignity", "Boujee" and "Look the part"; name plans by price.
5. Mark 2020 and modelled quotes as estimates on the card, and add a "paid this week" figure from riders.
6. Ship only Ntinda–CBD compare, Friday Jinja and Sunday Entebbe stages, circle-only or women-only; drop plug-ins and Bwindi until then.
