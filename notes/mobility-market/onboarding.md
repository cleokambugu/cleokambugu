# How others onboard drivers and riders

All *(snippet)* unless marked otherwise; the pages themselves were blocked from the session.
Gathered 2 September 2026 for UG v1's Handshake.

| Company | Driver onboarding as described | Rider side | Lesson for UG |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Uber** | Sign up in the Driver app or online; the form takes 15–20 minutes; upload licence, vehicle registration, insurance where required; in most cities a vehicle inspection; document review "up to 48 hours", often minutes to hours. | Phone, payment method, go. | Split "start" from "approved": let the driver finish in a minute and verify while they wait. |
| **Bolt (Kampala)** | Register online, upload documents, then attend a training and activation session at Bolt's office; own-vehicle drivers only. Uganda PSV: inspection at SGS centres (Kawanda, Nabingo, Namulanda, Namanve, Mbarara, Mbale, Gulu), certificate of fitness, third-party insurance, registration book, vetted police report. | Standard app. | The office visit is the bottleneck. UG's Vouch lets a driver earn inside a circle before the office step, and the document list should be shown as a checklist with the SGS centres named. |
| **SafeBoda** | Three years in the boda trade, a stage, national ID, an interview; a SafeBoda rep visits the stage and asks the chairman; the rider pays for training, two helmets, a smartphone, two vests. About 15% commission. | Helmet for the passenger. | The stage-chairman check is a vouch system already. Digitise it: chairman vouches in-app. |
| **Faras** | Boda and car; wallet, live tracking, in-app SOS; 10% commission. | Standard. | Commission is the recruiting message in Uganda. UG's pool fee (10% of the rider share) must be shown per trip in shillings, not as a percentage. |
| **BlaBlaCar** | Profile photo, a few words, phone; ID upload and social profiles for verification; publish a ride with route, time, seats and a suggested price per seat computed from fuel, wear and tolls, adjustable within a band; publish several dates at once; automatic or manual approval of passengers. | Verified profiles, ratings, "BlaBla" chattiness level. | The suggested price from a formula is exactly UG's pool split. Multi-date publishing becomes UG's comfort map: say the days once. |
| **inDrive** | Riders propose a fare; verified drivers accept, counter, or decline without penalty; commission often under 10%. Pitched in South Africa, Zambia, Nigeria on driver freedom and reaching areas conventional apps skip. | Rider sets price. | Negotiation sells in Africa. UG keeps the haggle for street bodas and special hires, and gives drivers a decline-without-penalty on stage offers. |
| **Swvl** | Company-run fleets on fixed and dynamic routes; riders book seats on buses and vans with fixed timings and rates; launched in Nairobi in 2019 as "an on-demand bus shuttle". | Book a seat on a route. | Fixed routes with a company fleet is capital-heavy. UG's Virtual Stage gets the same predictability from private cars by pooling paid demand first. |
| **Via** | Demand-responsive shared transit run for cities and companies; not researched beyond general knowledge. | | Same. |

## Regulation seen in the snippets

- Uganda PSV licence for cars: SGS inspection, certificate of fitness, third-party insurance,
  registration book, vetted police report (wikiprocedure).
- Boda PSV licence exists as a separate "public special motorcycle" category.
- Bolt Kampala requires the driver to own the vehicle.

## What none of them do

None pools *paid* demand on a corridor first and then offers a full, funded car to a private
driver who has only declared where they are comfortable going. Swvl needs a fleet; BlaBlaCar
needs the driver to post; inDrive negotiates one trip at a time. That gap is UG v1.
