## Intercity and stage fares (2026)

- Kampala–Jinja (2–3 h): UGX 20,000–30,000.
- Longer routes south-west or north: UGX 40,000–60,000.
- Link Bus is named as a major operator to Mbarara and other towns.

## Tolls

- Kampala–Entebbe Expressway: motorcycles over 400 cc UGX 3,000; light vehicles UGX 5,000;
  medium goods UGX 10,000; large goods and buses UGX 15,000; six or more axles UGX 18,000.
  Discounts: Easy Pass 10%; weekly pass 14 trips at 50% off; monthly pass 60 trips at 70% off.
- Kampala–Jinja Expressway: not built; tolls not set.

## Middle-class income

- A monthly income of UGX 1–5 million "was found appropriate" to define Uganda's middle class
  (KAS paper by Sophie Brandt).
- Upper-middle daily consumption US$10–20 per person (UGX 36,500–73,000).
- 8.3 million Ugandans (22%) middle class on 2016/17 data (EPRC).
- Kampala median household income about UGX 667,000; wider urban UGX 436,000 (Reall report).
- 23.5% of urban earners above UGX 1 million a month.

## Regulation

- Boda riders: Class M permit; PSV licence UGX 10,000 a year from the Transport Licensing
  Board; helmets required; KCCA boda-free zones in the CBD.
- Kampala's Lord Mayor: all bodas should operate under digital companies.
- Ride-hailing companies are licensed (Uber, Bolt, inDrive, Dial Jack named).
- Research (The Conversation, Uganda Business News, January 2026) finds platform work has not
  delivered formalisation or protection for boda drivers, and that app pricing pressures
  speed.
